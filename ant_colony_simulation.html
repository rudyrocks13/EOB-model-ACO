How ants find the shortest path

Every ant lays pheromone as it walks and picks its route by scent strength. Pheromone fades over time. Ants on the shorter branch finish their lap sooner, so their trail is refreshed more often — the trail wins not because ants "decide" it's shorter, but because it never gets the chance to fade.
